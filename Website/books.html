A Garden Lost

PublisherAmazon KDP / T.G. Brown Publishing
AuthorYenshio (T.G. Brown)
SettingPrague & Foundation HQ, 2030
StatusAvailable Now

For more than two thousand years, Eva and Adamocles Eden kept their secret. The Foundation they built became the world's most powerful philanthropic organization — and its most classified covert operation. Then Prague. A single operation that refused to stay contained. An investigation into a seemingly routine incident reveals a Sorrow Golem, the Black House's ritualistic footprint, and the first sign that their deepest circles have been penetrated.

A Garden Lost assembles the team — Cole, Sullivan, Bors — and introduces the Oni as an organized, coordinated threat. It sets every piece on the board for what follows.

"Beautiful view," Adamocles murmured, his gaze sweeping across the vista from the balcony... a subtle clink of boots on pavement pricked his awareness.

Yenshio

Book Two of the Eden Philanthropic Foundation Series

Gaia Project

PublisherAmazon KDP / T.G. Brown Publishing
SettingOslo · Prague · Frankfurt · Antarctica, 2030
Central ThreatThe Gaia Project / Akirl
StatusAvailable Now

It begins in Norway. Bors Adamsen — adopted son of the Edens, born to the cold — is ambushed in Oslo by Il'Shihane, the Red Kensei, and left for dead. The Foundation's Phoenix Protocol brings him back. But the assault was not random: it was the opening move in the Black House's most ambitious operation yet.

The Gaia Project is the Black House's plan to create Alpha-empowered hosts for Akirl — an ancient entity they intend to manifest on the material plane. Their method: a serum derived from a captive Alpha female wolf. The Foundation follows the trail from Prague to Frankfurt to a classified base in Antarctica, while Il'Shihane's psychic nullification abilities systematically strip away the Edens' most reliable advantages.

Mac enters the series here — Dillon Arthur MacMillan, former CIA, encountered by Eva in Prague during a Night Lords vampire operation. Kit Maker Stylez, the Foundation's tech genius, comes fully into his own. And Sullivan confronts the truth about her husband Marco's death.

"The biting cold of Oslo whipped through the forested wonderland... Bors Adamsen, a man born to this harsh environment, usually reveled in it."
— Opening, Book Two

Book Three, Part One of the Eden Philanthropic Foundation Series

The Host, Part 1

SettingParis, May 12 2031
Opens WithLa Seine Musicale Gala, Boulogne-Billancourt
PublisherAmazon KDP / T.G. Brown Publishing
StatusAvailable Now

The Host, Part 1 opens on May 12, 2031 in Paris — Eva performing at the La Seine Musicale gala, radiant in crimson, her psionic presence a beacon across a dome full of donors and dignitaries. Ado at her side, a formidable stillness, holding a surprise bouquet and a telepathic communication that has nothing to do with the mission briefing waiting in their earpieces.

The night barely ends before it begins again. Necroshot on the Seine. The Chairman's design revealed. The Face of Dominion. A Silence of God that means something specific in this series. And a Necrobomb that changes the operational math entirely.

Everything the previous books built is called in here. Every wound, every faction, every piece of ancient business the Edens have been deferring for centuries.

"The air at La Seine Musicale was electric, a symphony of light and sound that moved as one, a living thing. Eva, radiant in a deep crimson dress that flowed like liquid fire, stood behind a translucent console..."
— Prologue, The Host Part 1

Prequel to the Eden Philanthropic Foundation Series

Eva and Ado: The Beginning

PublisherAmazon KDP / T.G. Brown Publishing
AuthorYenshio (T.G. Brown)
TimelineOrigin — Before Book One
StatusAvailable Now

The origin story. Before the Foundation. Before Prague. Before centuries of war and survival and the particular weight of outliving every civilization they ever loved. Eva and Ado: The Beginning traces the creation of the bond that holds everything else together, told from the very first moment of Ado's existence, and the Creator's answer to a question he was almost too ashamed to ask.

A standalone entry point for readers who want the foundation beneath the Foundation before Book One begins.
