Book Three, Part One — Released

The Host, Part 1 Is Now Available

April 2026 Release News Yenshio

The Host, Part 1 opens on May 12, 2031, at La Seine Musicale in Paris, and within its first pages, the rules of the series change. A Temple Corp ambush. A Necroshot wound on Ado's left shoulder that does not fully heal. A global $50,000,000 bounty on the Foundation's core team. From the opening chapter, The Host makes clear that what worked before is no longer guaranteed to work again.

The Black House's six factions are fully coordinated for the first time. The Transposition Ritual, the Chairman's plan to bring Akirl into the physical world, is not coming. It has already begun. Part 1 is available now on Amazon in Kindle and paperback. Part 2 continues the story and is in active development.

The Black House: Six Factions, One Chairman

The Black House is not a single enemy. It is six coordinated factions under a Chairman whose identity remains unknown even to Eva and Ado. The Oni Hegemony. The Coterie of Sorcerers. The Illuminati/Black Templars and their Temple Corp military arm. The Caliphate. The Chaos Bureaucracy. And the Night Lords of Eastern Europe, who introduced themselves to the Foundation through a 2028 CIA station massacre in Prague.

Ongoing

Voice in the EPF Verse: How Characters Sound

Eva speaks Italian to Ado and Latin to the old things that still understand it. Ado's sentences go incomplete when he is doing his most precise thinking — the thought already done before the words catch up. Cole's Cockney inflections sharpen under stress and soften around the team. Sullivan's professional register is so exact it becomes its own form of emotion. Every character in the EPF Verse is an instrument in a specific key.
