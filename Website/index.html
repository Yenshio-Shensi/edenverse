What Is the Edenverse?

Ancient Power. Modern War. Immortal Stakes.

Eva and Adamocles Eden have lived for over two thousand years. Through dynasties and disasters, they built the Eden Philanthropic Foundation as both shield and sword — a private empire that operates in the spaces between governments and gods.

But the Oni — supernatural factions who have always known the Edens exist — have never been more organized. The Black House pulls strings from shadows. The Caliphate burns contracts across continents. And something older than any of them is waking up.
